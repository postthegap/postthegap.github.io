How to read this

  • A pick is posted only when a sportsbook's price pays more than the fair price on a regulated exchange, and it is posted before the game.
  • Fair when posted is the exchange's price at that moment. Fair at the close is its last price before the game started.
  • Beating the closing line means the posted price was still better than fair when the game began. Over time that matters more than any one result.
  • Nothing is removed from this page. Misses stay.
